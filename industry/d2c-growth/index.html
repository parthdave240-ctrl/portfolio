The problem

D2C revenue usually stalls in the same three places.

The scaling trap

Scaling ad spend skyrockets your CAC.

The symptom: Raising your paid ad spend causes CAC to skyrocket and ROAS to collapse.

Fixed byRigorous channel tests, strict spend rules, and creative frameworks that scale spend without breaking efficiency.
The leaky bucket

First-time buyers never come back.

The symptom: First-time buyers hunt discounts and leave, with zero automated repeat-purchase loops in place.

Fixed byAutomated email and CRM flows that turn a one-off discount hunter into a high-margin repeat customer.
The blind funnel

Traffic arrives, and nobody knows why it bounces.

The symptom: Traffic hits your product pages, but checkout conversion stalls and nobody can pinpoint why.

Fixed byFull-funnel conversion optimization and data tracking tied strictly to real revenue, not vanity metrics.

The framework

How it runs, month by month.

The 7-block strategic chain sets your core decisions (ICP, Positioning, Messaging, Collateral, Channels, Conversion Process, Pricing). These steps dictate how it gets executed.

Month 1

Foundation, audit and positioning

01

Audit & Establish Baseline

Complete GTM assessment, identifying immediate quick wins and setting 3-, 6-, and 12-month revenue goals.

02

ICP Realignment

Define your core buyer demographics, psychographics, and explicit anti-ICPs.

03

Positioning Architecture

Anchor your brand correctly (Activity, Use Case, Category, or Competitive Alternative) with sharp differentiators.

04

Master Messaging Framework

Build status-quo narratives, pain points, objection handling, and core product messaging blocks.

Months 2 & 3

Motion, assets and acquisition engine

05

Growth Motion Design

Scale what already works: take your existing acquisition channels (paid social, creator-led, search-driven, or hybrid) and design how to scale them.

06

Asset Production

Brief and direct your creative team or agency so your store, landing pages, ad creatives, and case studies match your positioning. Video ads and social static images are produced by your team or agency, not by us.

07

Customer Acquisition Engine

Build the system that brings in new buyers: high-intent paid social and search, creator partnerships and organic search, each with a clear goal and spend rule.

Months 4 & 5

Conversion, CRO, pricing and scaling

08

Conversion & Checkout Process

Streamline site architecture, frictionless checkouts, and high-margin bump offers.

09

CRO Coaching

Continuously review funnel drop-offs and test product page variations to compound conversion quality.

10

Pricing & Bundle Packaging

Optimize value metrics, bundles, multi-packs, and subscription tiers to maximize Average Order Value (AOV).

11

Channel Scaling

Scale spend and reach on the channels that already work, step by step, with spend rules that protect CAC.

Month 6

Retention and growth dashboards

12

Retention & Lifecycle Management

Build lifecycle management across email and WhatsApp (Welcome, Replenishment, Win-back) to drive repeat purchase, LTV and user-generated content.

13

Reporting & Growth Dashboards

Track weekly and monthly leading indicators across CAC, ROAS, AOV, LTV, and revenue.
