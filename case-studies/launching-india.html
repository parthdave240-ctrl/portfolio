D2CFULL FUNNEL

Launching India from zero

“The brand had no presence in India, no local pricing, no local positioning, and no evidence yet for what would actually resonate with a very different buyer.”

01

Started with research, not a translated version of the existing site

10 to 15 customer interviews before writing any creative, understanding the specific daily pain point driving purchase in India, distinct from the brand's home markets.

02

Repositioned around what the research actually found

Shifted from a premium, aspirational pitch to an everyday-utility one, once interviews showed Indian buyers saw this as a practical problem to solve, not a luxury to aspire to.

03

Built the full funnel and reported the honest result

Persona-based landing pages, a modeled conversion funnel, and PR that earned real unpaid national press pickup, alongside an honest account of where conversion still fell short of target.
