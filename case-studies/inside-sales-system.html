The brief for every human in it: relationship over sales. Build trust, answer the real question, and let the buyer make their own decision.

How it was built
01

Put AI in front of people, not instead of them

An AI-assisted layer ran on every booked call, filtering sales queries from support issues before either reached a live person. Callers spent their time on real purchase conversations, and chat support handled service questions, instead of both teams sorting through one mixed queue.

02

Called high-ticket cart abandoners within two hours

The moment a cart was abandoned, email and WhatsApp went out automatically through Klaviyo. In parallel, the calling team reached high-ticket abandoners personally within 2 hours, fast enough to catch genuine hesitation rather than idle curiosity.

03

Wrote a script for trust, not a discount close

The calling brief was to answer real questions and remove friction around financing, delivery timing and warranty. Callers used decision language (“you’re set to own it today”) instead of discount language (“here’s your $200 off”), the same framing that beat discount-led offers in paid ads and email. People picked up expecting a pitch and got a conversation instead.

04

Gave undecided visitors a reason to talk

Consultative landing pages carried a “request a callback” form instead of a bigger discount. It captured people who weren’t ready to buy but wanted answers first, and fed them straight to the calling team.

05

Reused the same system to launch a new market

For a from-scratch market launch, the model was rebuilt as an on-site AI agent handling product questions, pricing and demo booking, routing high-intent conversations to a live inside sales team, with WhatsApp follow-up and a dedicated calling team for abandoned carts.
