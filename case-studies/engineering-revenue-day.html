D2CPAID + EMAIL

Engineering the single biggest revenue day of the year

“The brand needed one day of the year to substantially outperform every other, without it being a one-off spike that left the rest of the month flat.”

01

Tested the offer's framing, not just its size

“Own it today” outperformed “Save $200” on the identical discount, on both click-through and conversion, reframing the purchase as a decision rather than a markdown.

02

Built a five-stage email sequence, each with one job

Teaser, early access, launch, last chance, and post-purchase upsell, a deliberate build rather than the same message repeated five times.

03

Synced urgency across every surface

The same live countdown ran on the landing page and inside the last-chance email, and paid budget shifted toward winning creative in real time during the event itself.
