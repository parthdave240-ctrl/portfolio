D2CEMAIL / LIFECYCLE

Turning email into a top-three revenue channel

“Email was running as a generic broadcast channel, with no clear sense of which sends actually drove revenue versus which just filled the calendar.”

01

Mirrored the same campaign-moment structure used on paid

Festive and cultural moments got dedicated email campaigns, not just dedicated landing pages, applying the same philosophy to the inbox.

02

Tested plain, personal copy against discount-led copy

A founder-voiced note with no discount outperformed every discount-led send that quarter, on both open and click rate.

03

Built recovery flows for the moments that mattered most

Abandoned-cart and post-checkout recovery flows, timed to the customer's actual behavior rather than a fixed send schedule.
