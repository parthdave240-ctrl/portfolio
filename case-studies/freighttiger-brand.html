B2B SAASPRODUCT POSITIONING

Giving Freight Tiger's AI product a brand and a story of its own

The stack and the funnel: ERP holds the record, the TMS runs the freight, TigerSight reads the data and decides, and the parent site funnels “AI” traffic straight to tigersight.ai.

“TigerSight, Freight Tiger's newest and most strategic product, had no clear home in the existing site, and needed to compete for attention against project44 and India's established visibility players while explaining a genuinely complex, 20-plus-agent product to very different buyers.”

01

Gave it a separate domain and a bolder category claim

Launched TigerSight on its own domain, tigersight.ai, positioned as ‘logistics' next chapter,’ so the AI story could be more ambitious than a TMS site allows while staying clearly part of Freight Tiger, the parent site carries operational credibility, the AI site carries the vision.

The flywheel the whole narrative rests on: execution creates data, AI turns it into decisions, and network intelligence makes every next decision stronger.

02

Explained it as a stack position, not a feature list

Positioned TigerSight as the third layer above the ERP, the system of record, and the TMS, the execution layer, reading live freight data, automating routine work, and surfacing only what genuinely needs a person.

03

Made the agents concrete, not abstract

Built the narrative around a live library of 20-plus agents, shown through specific examples: Sentry ranks and auto-resolves exceptions, Long Stoppage Voice Agents call an idle driver directly and update the ETA before anyone has to ask, Vehicle Procurement Voice Agents call every transporter on a lane at once instead of one at a time.

The agent library live on tigersight.ai: each agent gets its own tab, one plain-language line on what it does, and a working example. Shown here, the Document Aggregation Agent.

04

Structured 8 use cases as before/after, tagged by buyer

Planning through Backhaul Matching, each use case pairs today's pain, spreadsheets, detention found weeks later on an invoice, attention going to whoever calls loudest, with what TigerSight does instead, tagged Ground Team or CXO so each buyer immediately sees their own stake.

05

Packaged 20+ agents as one intelligence layer an enterprise can audit

For enterprise and strategic-partner conversations, reframed the agent library as “one intelligence layer for every freight decision”: four stacked layers (guardrails, a freight-grade data spine, a homegrown model-agnostic AI platform, and decisions that act), with the agents grouped into three swarms buyers can remember: Plan, Sentry and Optima.

06

Led with measured outcomes, not model names

Put five operational numbers at the front of the story, and showed the same AI working inside the marketplace: reading WhatsApp demand and voice-calling drivers in regional languages, so “AI-native” is something a buyer can see in daily volumes, not a claim.

The enterprise version of the story: four layers from guardrails to decisions that act, Mission Control as the visual proof, and measured outcomes underneath.
