D2C PAID MEDIA

Scaling spend 2x without losing a point of efficiency

Paid media, July to November 2025: spend, revenue and orders roughly doubled while blended ROAS and CAC held.

"The brand needed to scale paid spend hard into peak season, but every prior attempt at scaling in this category had seen efficiency collapse the moment budgets doubled."
01

Split the budget by job, not by platform

Search protected as the efficiency backbone; paid social run as a separate reach-and-retarget function with its own targets, rather than one blended CAC goal across both.

02

Weekly, not monthly, reporting

Spend, ROAS, CAC, and conversion tracked at the campaign level every week, catching efficiency drops inside days rather than at month end.

03

Protected the efficient channel while scaling the other

Search's budget grew in lockstep with results; paid social absorbed the more variable reach spend, so scaling one never came at the cost of the other's discipline.
